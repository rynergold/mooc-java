---

### ⚔️ Rules of Engagement (Methodology)

To ensure actual learning and not just copy-pasting, this repository follows strict Socratic rules:
1. **No Free Answers:** The AI mentor provides nudges and mental models, never raw implementation code.
2. **Mastery Scoring:** A topic is not marked "Done" in Notion until achieving a Mastery Score of 4 or 5 on custom extension drills. 
3. **Custom Verification:** The official MOOC tests are often broken. We verify logic by writing our own custom **JUnit 5** tests.
